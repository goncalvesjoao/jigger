# Rails application

Target: `jigger-rails/app/views/` and shared styles.
Mode: Operate. Cocktail background sections also serve Read mode.

## Scope

Review every public, account, personal-library, profile, and catalog administration page. Preserve the incumbent ivory/olive identity and Semanticus foundation. Preserve recipe prose, translations, credits, visibility rules, and cocktail/recipe relationships. The owner confirmed discovery and readable preparation as the priority, followed by authoring and organization.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Help someone find a drink and follow its preparation without distraction. Refine the established product UI; no replacement identity or concept tournament.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory background, near-white working surfaces, olive actions, dark green text, system sans-serif, restrained borders and rounded corners. Native form controls remain recognizable.

STORY: Search by name or available items; open a cocktail or recipe; gather ingredients and follow the method; save recipes reversibly to collections. Authoring makes its current limits explicit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Discovery leads with a compact task heading, search and availability controls, then photographs and results. Detail pages lead with the drink identity, contextual description and photo, followed by linked ingredients and preparation. Personal pages place the title and principal action together. Phone layouts stack without horizontal page scroll.

FORM: Existing application structure and identity, refined directly. No replacement-world seed or approved comp applies. Signature interaction: visible ingredient and step checkboxes, live completion counts, and reset actions. Motion conveys interaction state and respects reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Limits

Ingredient and utensil editing are not yet supported in the public recipe editor; users can save drafts and existing requirements are preserved. No queue, substitutions, taste filters or unsupported product claims are introduced. Existing seed photography is retained; no shipping raster is created or replaced.
