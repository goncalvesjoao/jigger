# Canonical cocktail seed sources

Researched 2026-11-01. `canonical_cocktails.json` contains original English summaries for the 30 names in the cached `cocktails.json`. The existing cache remains the source of imported recipe quantities and instructions. This file records evidence scope and editorial choices; it is not loaded by the application.

## Evidence and dates

- IBA pages define current cocktail specifications; they do not prove origin stories.
- Digitised historical bar manuals establish that a named recipe existed by the stated publication year. They do not establish first publication or invention.
- Restaurant, hotel and ingredient-producer origin accounts are attributed in the histories. They are primary evidence of those organisations’ claims, not independent proof of priority.
- `source_published_on` is deliberately omitted: the research established years or approximate periods, not exact publication dates. Those limitations are recorded in `source_date_note`. Database modification dates and access dates are not historical publication dates.
- TheCocktailDB is a community recipe catalogue. For less documented variations, it establishes the listed name and formulation; it does not establish a creator or invention date. Unknown origins are stated explicitly rather than filled with a legend.

## Recipe data differences retained

Canonical summaries describe the drink identity without rewriting imported recipes. The cached Espresso Martini ingredient list omits espresso although the IBA includes it. The Moscow Mule cache lists ginger ale while its own instructions and the IBA specify ginger beer. French Negroni uses Lillet in the cache and omits Campari. Banana Strawberry Shake Daiquiri contains fruit and apple juice with no spirit, so its description explicitly says alcohol-free. Sidecar Cocktail is described as a brandy sour without suggesting a separately proven origin. These existing recipe records were not silently normalised.

## Limits

This is a bounded seed-content research pass, not an exhaustive historical bibliography. The obscure variants do not receive invented heritage. Several live catalogue pages could not be fetched; those entries rely on the supplied cached records, as marked above. All prose is paraphrased; exact recipe text, photographs and source-page licensing are outside this content pass.
