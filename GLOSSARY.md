# Jigger

Jigger helps people discover cocktail recipes and find cocktails they can make.

## Language

**Cocktail**:
A canonical drink identity shared by recipes that make that cocktail, with its accepted names and history.
_Avoid_: Recipe, user cocktail

**Recipe**:
A particular way to make a drink, including ingredients, quantities and preparation instructions. A recipe may belong to a cocktail or remain unassigned, and always belongs to an author.

**Unassigned recipe**:
A recipe without a canonical cocktail classification. A complete public recipe remains discoverable as its own result until curated.

**Marketplace**:
The public collection of complete, published recipes, presented as individual recipes with author handles and links to the exact preparation. Recipe and linked cocktail names are searchable; private recipes and drafts are excluded.

**Technique**:
A reusable bartending action, such as shaking, stirring or fine-straining, with a description and instructions. A recipe may use several techniques; each has a beginner guide available from the preparation section.
